About the Project

Ethiopia Statistical Service (ESS)

This project is part of an internship initiative at the Ethiopia Statistical Service. The goal is to leverage modern data science benchmarks to improve climatic forecasting within the region.

Methodology

We employed a Random Forest Regressor algorithm due to its robustness in handling non-linear relationships in meteorological data.

  • Data Collection: 5 years of household and geospatial records.
  • Preprocessing: StandardScaler normalization and handling missing values.
  • Feature Engineering: Selection of top 20 most impactful variables.
  • Evaluation: Validated using K-Fold cross-validation.
